## Design Context

### Users

Students and young professionals aged 18–28 who want to meet like-minded people without the friction of traditional social discovery. Their goal is to close the loneliness gap by finding a compatible, algorithmically matched small group (3-5 people) for shared real-world activities through a single click ("Forge my group").

### Brand Personality

**The Catalyst** – the force that brings the right elements together and steps back.

- **Voice/Tone:** Knowledgeable peer, affirming, confident, warm, and direct. Never corporate.
- **Personality:** Intelligent but not intellectual; Warm but not sentimental; Confident but not arrogant; Modern but not trendy.
- **Emotional Goals:** Trust, belonging, and intentionality.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Structured Warmth**

- **Visual Tone:** Contemporary, clean, and highly deliberate with a purposeful dark-light scrolling alternation on public pages and a black-forward, long-session app shell.
- **Colors:** Forge Teal (`#0D9488`) and Spark Amber (`#F59E0B` light / `#FBBF24` dark) remain the core identity accents. The app uses a semantic surface ladder: light porcelain Canvas (`#F7F8F4`), near-white Card (`#FFFEFA`), tactile Input (`#EEF2ED`), Ink (`#1C1F1D`), Slate (`#68756F`), plus dark graphite surfaces (`#0B0F0E`, `#111716`, `#18201E`, `#202927`). Teal and Amber serve as purposeful accents (max 15% surface area).
- **Typography:** Exclusively **Inter**.
- **Anti-References:** No dating app language/mechanics ("match", "swipe"), no gamification ("level up"), no ML/algorithm jargon facing the user, no emojis as icons, no sharp corners on interactive UI, and no glassmorphism on light backgrounds.

### Design Principles

1. **Intentionality & Accessibility:** Every connection and interface element must feel purposeful and effortless. Implement 3-tap workflows, guided onboarding, and never use random or decorative motion.
2. **Rounded Warmth & Trust:** Maintain consistent border-radius guidelines (`rounded-2xl` for cards, `rounded-full` for pills/avatars) to project approachability without being overly sentimental. Trust is earned, visible, and moral.
3. **Hide the Complexity:** The matching algorithm is highly advanced (MBTI, OCEAN, trust scoring), but the user experience should be seamless and jargon-free (intelligent but not intellectual).
4. **Purposeful Motion:** Any animations or micro-interactions (e.g., hover states, node canvas background, fade-ups) must communicate intelligence and delight, supporting the "Wow" factor rather than serving as mere decoration.
