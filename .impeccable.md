## Design Context

### Users

Students and young professionals aged 18–28 who want to meet people through shared activities. They use "Forge my group" to ask TeamForge to form one small group around a plan.

### Brand Personality

**The Catalyst** – TeamForge helps form the group, then lets the members take over.

- **Voice/Tone:** Knowledgeable peer, affirming, confident, warm, and direct. Never corporate.
- **Personality:** Clear but not technical; warm but not sentimental; confident but not arrogant; modern but not trendy.
- **Emotional Goals:** Trust, belonging, and intentionality.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Structured Warmth**

- **Visual Tone:** Contemporary and clean, with alternating dark and light sections on public pages and dark surfaces in the app shell.
- **Colors:** Forge Teal (`#0D9488`) and Spark Amber (`#F59E0B` light / `#FBBF24` dark) remain the core identity accents. The app uses a semantic surface ladder: light porcelain Canvas (`#F7F8F4`), near-white Card (`#FFFEFA`), tactile Input (`#EEF2ED`), Ink (`#1C1F1D`), Slate (`#68756F`), plus dark graphite surfaces (`#0B0F0E`, `#111716`, `#18201E`, `#202927`). Use Teal and Amber as accents covering no more than 15% of a screen.
- **Typography:** Exclusively **Inter**.
- **Anti-References:** No dating app language/mechanics ("match", "swipe"), no gamification ("level up"), no ML/algorithm jargon facing the user, no emojis as icons, no sharp corners on interactive UI, and no glassmorphism on light backgrounds.

### Design Principles

1. **Clarity & Accessibility:** Give every control a clear purpose, keep common workflows short, guide users through onboarding, and do not add decorative motion.
2. **Rounded Surfaces:** Use `rounded-2xl` for cards and `rounded-full` for pills and avatars. Describe trust as a record of reliability, not a judgment of character.
3. **Keep Technical Details Out of UI Copy:** Group formation uses personality, interests, and trust data. Explain the user outcome without formulas or scoring jargon.
4. **Useful Motion:** Use animation to show state changes, progress, or relationships. Do not add motion only for decoration.
